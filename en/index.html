Frequently asked questions

10 questions

Yoi explains how things work; pricing, permissions, and data questions get a plain answer from the developer.

Q01What is the debuff value?▸
YOIIt's the time you've been awake, converted into drunkenness. Runs from 0 to 150. Stay up 17 hours straight and your judgment is about as sharp as at 0.05% blood alcohol — that comes from a study by Williamson & Feyer (2000). As the number climbs, I start looking worse, too.
Q02How does it go back down?▸
YOISleep. That's the whole trick. Seven hours means a full recovery; anything less heals only a fraction — your sleep divided by seven hours. A one-hour nap after an all-nighter is... well, a gesture.
Q03Are records automatic or manual?▸
YOIManual at its core. Tap the sleep button when you head to bed, tap the wake button when you're up. If you allow Apple Health access, the app can also pull in sleep data automatically whenever you open it. That link is optional, and you can turn it on later.
Q04What notifications will I get?▸
YOIIf you're still up late at night, I send warnings. A little pushy, I know — it's my job. In the morning you just get the result, stated flatly. Both are local notifications built on your device; nothing comes from a server.
Q05What is Bedtime Shield?▸
Bedtime Shield limits access to apps you choose, during hours you set. It is free. Because it uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls), iOS will ask for Screen Time permission the first time you enable it. The apps you select are stored only as anonymous tokens issued by iOS, kept on your device — the developer cannot learn which apps you picked.
Q06How much is free?▸
Everything that gets you to bed is free: the debuff display, night warnings and morning reports, the weekly wake schedule, date-specific overrides, Bedtime Shield, sleep records, day-by-day detail for the last 7 days, and 30-day charts and summaries. There are no ads.
Q07What does Pro unlock?▸
A single one-time purchase (¥200 in Japan; local pricing may vary) unlocks day-by-day detail older than 7 days, month-over-month comparison, and notification customization. It is not a subscription — no monthly fee, no renewal.
Q08Are there ads?▸
No. The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.
Q09Where is my data stored?▸
Everything stays on your device. There is no backend server and no account system, and the developer has no way to access your data. See the privacy policy for details.
Q10What are the requirements?▸
An iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later.
